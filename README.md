# Ant Venmo

[I send $0.01 to one of my friends money every hour, every day, for "ants".](https://account.venmo.com/u/nisalakalupahana) Venmo doesn't have a working public API, so this lets me do it.

### Why?

1. Paying for ants is just funny.
2. I've always found it funny (and a little sad) that Venmo has a social media feed. They, of course, want to steal your attention and get you to spend more time on the app. So how do we fight back? By completely destroying the feed. Every single of my friends' Venmo feeds (and the friends of whoever the bot is pointed at) is completely unusable now, filled with messages about ants.
3. On the flip side, nobody has really utilized Venmo's social media features in the positive direction either. If you can find a few highly-connected people on Venmo in a certain area, you could probably spend relatively little money and broadcast a message to them and everyone they're connected to. Could be great viral marketing.
